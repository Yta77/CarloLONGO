Expertise

Services

A focused set of skills developed over 15+ years across commodity trading, contract pricing and market risk management in global markets.

Market Risk Management
Design and ongoing oversight of market risk frameworks for commodity portfolios — energy, oil, metals and agricultural products. Daily monitoring of VaR (historical and parametric), Expected Shortfall, Mark-to-Market and Cash-Flow at Risk. Position limit definition, breach escalation procedures and board-level risk reporting.
Hedging Strategy
Development and implementation of hedging programmes for physical and financial commodity exposures. Instrument selection (futures, swaps, options, structured products), hedge ratio optimisation, effectiveness testing and ongoing P&L attribution. Tailored to the company's risk appetite and regulatory constraints.
Risk Management Setup
End-to-end design and implementation of risk management functions for companies entering or expanding in commodity markets. Covers risk policy drafting, KRI definition and limits, risk committee governance, reporting infrastructure (Excel and Python), and operational workflows — from zero to a fully functioning risk desk.
ETRM Implementation
Support across the full ETRM lifecycle — from vendor evaluation and requirements definition to system configuration, data migration and go-live. Experience with major platforms in energy and commodity markets. Particular focus on risk module setup: position management, VaR engines, MtM curves and reporting integration with finance and front office.
Quantitative Analytics & Tooling
Development of Python-based tools for commodity risk and trading operations — automated risk reporting pipelines, Monte Carlo contract pricing models, volatility surface construction, correlation analysis and position monitoring dashboards. Built for real-world use: scheduled runs, Outlook/email integration, interactive Dash front-ends and clean data outputs for risk committees.
